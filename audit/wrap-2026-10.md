# wrap steps — the recursive proof to ≤ 64 KB, 2026-10-10

Last gate of the proof-system repair (soft3
`proposals/proof-system-repair.md`): the constant-size recursive proof of
`audit/recursion-2026-10.md` (282–285 KB, verify 18–38 ms) shrunk by
proving its final verifier. Contract: `specs/recursion.md` § final
verifier, § field-native WHIR, § wrap; ledger: `specs/soundness.md`
§ field-native WHIR and wrap.

Stand: zheng `feat/wrap` (on `feat/ivc` 3148a42, with
`feat/machine-opcodes` 9d758ed merged), lens `feat/field-native-whir` (on
`feat/accumulation`). Apple M4 Max, 16 cores, 48 GB, rustc 1.95.0,
`--release`. The machine was shared with other agents (load 13–40);
times are single runs (verify: median of 5 or 11) and upper bounds of a
quiet machine. Sizes are deterministic.

## 3. where the bytes and the time are

Final proof, 62,589 B: header 49, nox-public claim 1,272 (`n + 37` Fp3:
statement data the verifier evaluates), AIR 4,736 (zerocheck 14 × 9 Fp3,
root, OOD, 49 columns at ρ, the 20 successor columns the constraints read),
opening 56,532 (rounds of 25 / 18 / 15 queries at rates 1/256, 1/2048,
1/16384; final polynomial 2^8 Fp3).

Verify (add.tri, `ZHENG_TIMING`): the opening ≈ 9–12 ms (round-0 leaf
sponges and paths, ≈ 1,000 permutations natively; the wiring weight's
closing term over 2^20 cells), the constraints' graph 1.5 ms, the key's
columns at ρ 1.5 ms, the nox-public claim and the public digest < 1 ms.

Prove: the final level's opening dominates (grinding 30 bits per round,
2–4 rounds; 5–12 min), then the 1/256 inner level (3–5 min).

## 5. what is left

- **verify ≤ 1 ms**: missed by ×12–15. The opening's hashing (≈ 1,000
  permutations, single-threaded) and the wiring weight's pass over the
  word are the floor of this design at today's hemera speed; candidates:
  batching the final opening's Merkle checks (the step verifier's batched
  mode is not used by the direct opening yet), a structured wiring
  (`u_λ` with a closed-form MLE), fewer round-0 queries (rate 1/1024 at
  ≈ 4 GB more prover memory).
- prover memory 32–38 GB and 13–20 min for the wrap chain: the 1/256
  levels' Fp3 combinations and codewords; unoptimised.
- the deferred nox-public claim travels (1,272 B); in-circuit evaluation
  needs the statement's init entries as bounded witness.
- no envelope profile for wrapped proofs (joy).
- no external review of the wrap circuit or of the linear-wiring final
  mode; the tests check them against the native verifier and tampering.
